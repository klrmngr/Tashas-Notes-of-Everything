###### The Drowned Crown
<span class="sub2">:FasSun: DAY 01 &nbsp; | &nbsp; :FasTags: `VIEW[{tags}][text]`</span>
___

> [!quote|no-t] SUMMARY
> A one-shot adventure taking the party from the Elven Harbour through a Drow warship intercept and deep into a desert ruin — to face the undying Sea Lich known as **The Drowned Eternal** and claim (or destroy) the crown it guards.

---

## Act 1 — Getting There
> *Maps: Elven Harbour → Drow Warship*

The party departs from the **Elven Harbour**, where the dockmaster has intel on the Drow fleet's heading. They can steal or hire a vessel, but a **Drow patrol ship** intercepts them mid-journey.

> [!important] Objectives
> - Board and fight through the Drow Warship *(night variant)*
> - Clear the path forward
> - Capture a **navigator** who knows where the crown is hidden
> - Potentially **commandeer the ship**

> [!example|flex] Encounters
> - Drow sailors
> - Drow lieutenant
> - Chain Devil *(bound as ship guardian)*

---

## Act 2 — The Ruins
> *Maps: Ancient Stepwell → Tomb of Sand → Tomb of Sand Interior*

The crown lies in a partially submerged desert ruin complex. The **Ancient Stepwell** serves as the entrance — flooded lower levels, traps, and territorial creatures. Descend into the **Tomb of Sand** exterior, then push into the **Tomb of Sand Interior** where the crown rests in a sealed burial chamber.

> [!warning] The tomb isn't empty.

> [!example|flex] Encounters
> - Harpies / Water Weird *(Stepwell entrance)*
> - Tomb guardians
> - See: **The Sea Lich** below

---

## Boss — The Sea Lich "The Drowned Eternal"
> *Reflavored Necromancer + Custom Phases*

> [!danger] PHASE 1 — The Tide Rises *(CR 9)*
> **Base:** Necromancer (CR 9) statblock. The lich appears partially submerged in a ritual pool, still completing a dark ceremony. Fights **defensively**, hiding behind minions.
>
> **Minions:**
> - 4–6 Skeletons dressed as ancient temple guards
> - 2 Ghouls that rise from the water at the **start of round 2**
> - 1 Ghost that phases through the temple walls periodically
>
> **Phase ends** when the lich drops to half HP — it screams, shatters the ritual pool, and floods part of the floor with ankle-deep cursed water. *Difficult terrain everywhere.*

> [!danger] PHASE 2 — The Phylactery Revealed *(CR 11–12)*
> The lich sheds its decayed flesh and reveals its true form — a **barnacle-encrusted skeleton** with glowing deep-sea blue eye sockets.
>
> **Boosts:**
> - Legendary Resistance (2/day)
> - **Paralyzing Touch** — DC 14 Con save or paralyzed until end of next turn
> - **3 Legendary Actions:** cantrip / move without provoking OA / cast spell up to 3rd level
>
> **Phylactery:** A barnacle-covered nautilus shell on the altar, glowing faintly. Visible with a Perception or Arcana check. **40 HP, AC 15** — must be destroyed to prevent regeneration.
>
> **New Hazard:** Cursed water on the floor deals **1d6 necrotic damage** to anyone who ends their turn standing in it (undead are immune).

> [!danger] PHASE 3 — The Deep Awakens *(CR 13–14)*
> Triggers when the lich drops to **25% HP** or the phylactery is destroyed. Desperate and dangerous.
>
> - Casts **Bestow Curse** or **Blight** as a bonus action once per phase
> - Ceiling cracks open — **2 Merrow Skeletons** drop in *(Merrow statblock + undead immunities)*
> - Starts targeting **downed players** with Paralyzing Touch to prevent revives
> - If phylactery is still intact: lich regains **20 HP** at the start of each of its turns

---

## Temple Features

> [!info] Ritual Pillars
> Provide **half cover**, but crumble if they take 20+ damage. Collapse onto nearby creatures: *Dex save DC 13, 2d10 bludgeoning.*

> [!info] The Altar
> A player who spends an **action studying it** learns what the phylactery is and where it is.

> [!info] Rising Water *(Phase 3)*
> Water rises **1 foot per round** in phase 3 — incentivizes urgency and action economy pressure.
